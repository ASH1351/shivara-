# 👑 SHIVARA BY RIDDHI
> *"Tradition Worn With Grace"*

A luxury, high-end heritage jewelry website for **SHIVARA BY RIDDHI** — an Indian fine jewelry house specializing in handcrafted 22K gold necklaces, certified natural Basra pearls, bridal polki chokers, and sacred temple jewellery.

Inspired by the timeless aesthetics of **Tanishq Heritage × Cartier High Jewelry × Mikimoto Pearls**.

## 🏛️ Website Features

- **Heritage Page Loader:** Gold Trishul and rotating pearl wreath drawing animation.
- **Hero Canvas Atmosphere:** 3D floating iridescent pearls and shimmering gold dust particles (auto-throttled on mobile for 60fps).
- **Sticky Glassmorphism Navbar:** Transparent on hero, transitioning into ivory cream blur with search, wishlist, cart badges, and appointment booking.
- **Multi-Currency Converter:** Real-time conversion across **₹ INR**, **$ USD**, **£ GBP**, and **AED**.
- **Signature Collections:** Bridal Necklaces, Pearl Necklaces, Gold Choker Sets, Temple Jewellery, Daily Wear Elegance, and The Festive Edit.
- **Interactive Catalog:** Category tabs, dual-view on hover, price range slider (₹80K to ₹10L), gemstone filter, and sorting.
- **Product Quick View Modal:** Multi-angle image thumbnails, craftsmanship specifications table (Gold Purity, Net/Gross Weight, Gems, Certification), and direct WhatsApp buy.
- **Brand Story ("By Riddhi"):** Editorial portrait with 4 pearl corner studs, 5th-generation Karigari narrative, and founder signature.
- **Interactive Neckline & Layering Guide:** Match bridal lehengas, silk sarees, and evening gowns with optimal necklace pairings.
- **Customer Testimonials Carousel:** Quotes, star ratings, verified bride credentials, and city tags.
- **Working Bag & Wishlist:** `localStorage` persistence, quantity stepper, subtotal calculation, and **"Order via WhatsApp Concierge"** pre-formatted checkout.
- **Green-Free Gold Floating WhatsApp Button:** Custom gold metallic circle with white icon for strict luxury consistency.
- **Mobile Responsive Bottom Bar:** Fixed bar on screens ≤ 768px (Home, Shop, Wishlist, Cart, WhatsApp).
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Semantic HTML5, OpenGraph meta tags, and JSON-LD schema for a `JewelryStore`.
