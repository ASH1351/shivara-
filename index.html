Crafted With Tradition, Worn With Grace

Shivara was born from a singular reverent vision by founder Riddhi — to preserve India’s timeless temple and royal jewelry traditions, reimagined for the modern queen. Each necklace begins as an archival sketch inspired by royal Marwar dynasties and Vedic sacred symmetry.

Handcrafted by 5th-generation master karigars in our private Jaipur and Benares ateliers, our pieces embody patience, devotion, and uncompromising purity. When you drape a Shivara necklace upon your collarbone, you do not simply wear jewelry — you carry a living heirloom of grace.
